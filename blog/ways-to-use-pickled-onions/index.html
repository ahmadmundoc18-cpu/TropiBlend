12 Ways to Use Pickled Onions (Beyond Burgers)

September 29, 2026 · 2 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

Pickled onions add crunch, tang and color to almost any savory dish. Use them on burgers and sandwiches, with inihaw and lechon manok, on fried chicken, in tacos, beside tapsilog and other silog meals, on salads and avocado toast, and on pizza or nachos. TropiBlend Pickled Onion comes ready to eat in a 350 ml jar.

A jar of pickled onions is one of the easiest ways to make everyday food taste more exciting. TropiBlend Pickled Onion rings are bright pink, tangy and crisp. Here are twelve ways to use them.

With Filipino favorites

  1. Inihaw na liempo or pork BBQ. The tang cuts through smoky, fatty pork. See the best condiments for inihaw.
  2. Lechon manok and fried chicken. A few rings beside the chicken work better than another dip. Pair them with pickled cucumber for extra crunch.
  3. Tapsilog, longsilog, tocilog. Pickled onion on the side balances salty-sweet breakfast meats. See our silog toppings guide.
  4. Grilled fish. Layer rings over grilled bangus or tilapia with a squeeze of calamansi.
  5. Sisig. Add a pile on top just before serving for crunch and brightness.

In sandwiches and bread

  1. Burgers. Together with pickled cucumber, this is our TropiBlend burger.
  2. Sandwiches. Try them in chicken, tuna or egg sandwiches.
  3. Hotdogs. Swap raw onion for pickled rings.

Beyond

  1. Tacos and burritos. Pickled onion is a classic taco topping.
  2. Salads. Use as a colorful topping, and a splash of the pickling liquid can go into your dressing.
  3. Avocado toast. Creamy avocado with tangy onion is a great contrast.
  4. Pizza and nachos. Scatter over after baking.

Pickled onions are also the easiest way to make a plate look good. That pop of pink makes any dish look finished.
