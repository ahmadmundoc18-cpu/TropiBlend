Best Condiments for Inihaw: Beyond Toyomansi and Suka

September 29, 2026 · 3 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

The best condiments for inihaw balance smoky, salty grilled meat with something tangy and fresh. Alongside the classic toyomansi and spiced vinegar, try pickled onion and pickled cucumber with liempo and pork BBQ, pineapple salsa with pork, and mango salsa with grilled fish and chicken. Serve two or three so everyone can mix and match.

Inihaw (or sinugba, as many Kagay-anons say) is all about smoke and char. What goes beside it matters just as much as the grilling. Toyomansi and sukang pinakurat will always have a place, but a few fresh, tangy additions make the whole spread better.

Why tangy condiments work with grilled food

Grilled meat is smoky, salty and often fatty. Acidic, slightly sweet sides reset your palate between bites, so the tenth bite of liempo tastes as good as the first. That's the same reason atchara and ensaladang mangga have always been served with grilled food.

What to serve with each

Inihaw na liempo: Pickled Onion or Pineapple Salsa. The tang and sweetness balance the fatty pork. More ideas: 12 ways to use pickled onions.

Pork BBQ sticks: Pickled Cucumber for a cool crunch, or Pineapple Salsa for a sweet-tangy contrast. See what to eat with pineapple salsa.

Chicken inasal or grilled chicken: Mango Salsa or Pickled Cucumber. See pickled cucumber vs atchara.

Grilled fish (bangus, tilapia, tuna panga): Mango Salsa spooned right on top, or Pickled Onion with calamansi. See grilled fish with fruit salsa.

Grilled squid and seafood: Mango Salsa.

Grilled vegetables and eggplant: Pickled Onion or a drizzle of Mango Salad Dressing.

Build a sawsawan board

For a family grill or barkada gathering, set out small bowls of:

  1. Toyomansi (soy sauce and calamansi)
  2. Spiced vinegar
  3. TropiBlend Pickled Onion
  4. TropiBlend Pickled Cucumber
  5. TropiBlend Mango or Pineapple Salsa

Guests can build their own combinations.

Put condiments out in small bowls with their own spoons instead of passing the jar around. The jar stays clean and fresh for next time.

Frequently asked questions

What goes well with inihaw na liempo?

Tangy sides like pickled onion, pickled cucumber, atchara and pineapple salsa, alongside toyomansi or spiced vinegar and plenty of rice.

What salsa goes with grilled pork?

Pineapple salsa is a classic match for grilled pork. Mango salsa works too.

What's a good side dish for Filipino BBQ?

Pickles like pickled cucumber, pickled onion or atchara, fresh salsa, ensaladang talong or a simple cucumber-tomato salad.
