What Is TropiBlend? Gourmet Condiments Made in Cagayan de Oro

September 29, 2026 · 3 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

TropiBlend is a Filipino food brand from Cagayan de Oro City that makes six small-batch gourmet condiments: Pineapple Salsa, Mango Salsa, Pickled Cucumber, Pickled Onion, Pesto and Mango Salad Dressing. Everything is made from fresh and natural ingredients with no artificial preservatives, and you can order directly through Facebook Messenger, by phone or by email.

If you've spotted a jar with the little "tb" leaf logo at a fair in CDO or on your feed, this is the short version of who we are and what's inside those jars.

A Kagay-anon condiment brand

TropiBlend is a Filipino food brand based in Cagayan de Oro City, the City of Golden Friendship. We make gourmet condiments that bring bright, tangy, tropical flavor to the food Filipinos already love, from inihaw and fried chicken to pasta, sandwiches and fresh salads.

Our tagline says it best: made from fresh and natural ingredients. Every jar starts with real fruit, vegetables and herbs, blended in small batches with simple pantry ingredients like lime, cane sugar, salt and spices. There are no artificial preservatives.

The six TropiBlend products

  • Pineapple Salsa (500 ml or 350 ml): sweet pineapple with bell pepper, onion and lime. See what to eat with pineapple salsa.
  • Mango Salsa (500 ml or 350 ml): ripe mango with bell pepper, onion and lime. See what to eat with mango salsa.
  • Pickled Cucumber (500 ml or 350 ml): crunchy Japanese cucumber with dill and lime. See pickled cucumber vs atchara.
  • Pickled Onion (350 ml or 200 ml): bright pink, tangy onion rings. See 12 ways to use pickled onions.
  • Pesto (350 ml or 200 ml): basil, parmesan, extra virgin olive oil and cashew nuts. See how to use pesto.
  • Mango Salad Dressing (350 ml or 150 ml): silky mango and lime with a gentle pepper finish.

You can see photos, full ingredient lists and pairing ideas for each one on our products page.

What makes TropiBlend different

Tropical fruit, used generously. Mango and pineapple are the heart of our salsas and dressing, not just a flavoring.

Made for Filipino meals. We don't think of condiments as fancy extras. Pickled cucumber next to your lechon manok, mango salsa on grilled bangus, pesto on pandesal: that's how we eat them at home.

Nothing artificial. Every ingredient is listed on the label, and there are no artificial preservatives in any of them.

Proudly local. Everything is crafted in CDO, and we sell directly to Kagay-anon families, so every order supports a local small business.
