Are TropiBlend Products All Natural? What's Really in Each Jar

October 4, 2026 · 2 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

Yes. Every TropiBlend product is made from fresh and natural ingredients with no artificial preservatives. The salsas are mostly fruit and vegetables with lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices; the pesto is basil, parmesan, extra virgin olive oil and cashew nuts. The full ingredient list is printed on every label.

"All natural" gets printed on a lot of labels, so we'd rather show you. Here is exactly what goes into each TropiBlend product.

The full ingredient lists

Pineapple Salsa: pineapple, bell pepper, onion, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

Mango Salsa: mango, bell pepper, onion, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

Pickled Cucumber: Japanese cucumber, dill, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

Pickled Onion: onion, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

Pesto: basil, parmesan cheese, extra virgin olive oil, cashew nuts, herbs and spices.

Mango Salad Dressing: mango, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

What "natural" means at TropiBlend

Real produce first. The first ingredient in our salsas and dressing is the fruit itself, so you're getting actual mango and pineapple, not flavoring.

Pantry ingredients only. Lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices are ingredients you'd find in your own kitchen.

No artificial preservatives. We don't add artificial preservatives to any of our products. That's also why we ask you to keep the jars refrigerated. See how to store TropiBlend after opening.

Small batches. We make our condiments in small batches in Cagayan de Oro rather than in huge factory runs.

Reading the label

Every jar lists its ingredients, net volume and best-before date. If you have a question about an ingredient, or you're cooking for someone with dietary needs, just message us. For allergens, see our allergen and dietary guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does TropiBlend use artificial preservatives?

No. None of the six TropiBlend products contain artificial preservatives.

Do TropiBlend salsas contain real fruit?

Yes. Pineapple is the first ingredient in the Pineapple Salsa and mango is the first ingredient in both the Mango Salsa and the Mango Salad Dressing.

What sweetener does TropiBlend use?

The salsas, pickles and mango dressing use cane sugar. The pesto has no added sugar listed in its ingredients.
