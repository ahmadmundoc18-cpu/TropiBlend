How to Order TropiBlend: Messenger, Phone, Email or Form

September 29, 2026 · 3 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

To order TropiBlend, send the products and quantities you want by Facebook Messenger (TropiBlendph), call or text 0956 447 9961, email elsie@tropiblend.com, or use the order form on tropiblend.com. We reply to confirm availability, price and pickup or delivery details in Cagayan de Oro.

In this article

  1. Step 1: Choose your products
  2. Step 2: Send us your order
  3. Step 3: Include these details
  4. Step 4: We confirm, then you enjoy
  5. Frequently asked questions

Ordering TropiBlend takes about a minute. There's no account to create and no checkout page. You just tell us what you want and we confirm it with you.

Step 1: Choose your products

Our line-up has six products. Here are the sizes to use when you order:

  • Pineapple Salsa, 500 ml jar
  • Mango Salsa, 500 ml jar
  • Pickled Cucumber, 500 ml jar
  • Pickled Onion, 350 ml jar
  • Pesto, 300 ml jar
  • Mango Salad Dressing, 350 ml bottle

Not sure which ones? Our products page has pairing ideas for each, and what is TropiBlend? gives a quick tour of the range. If you're ordering for a grill night, start with the best condiments for inihaw.

Step 2: Send us your order

Pick whichever channel is easiest for you:

  1. Facebook Messenger: message TropiBlendph. This is usually the fastest.
  2. Call or text: 0956 447 9961.
  3. Email: elsie@tropiblend.com.
  4. Website form: tick the products on the order form, add quantities and press send. It opens your email app with everything filled in.

Step 3: Include these details

To get your order confirmed quickly, include:

  • Your name and mobile number
  • Products and quantities, for example "2 Mango Salsa, 1 Pesto"
  • Your barangay or area in CDO (for example Carmen, Lapasan, Bulua)
  • When you need it, especially for parties and gifts

Ordering for an event? Tell us the date up front so we can plan the batch for you.

Step 4: We confirm, then you enjoy

We'll reply to confirm availability, price and the pickup or delivery arrangement. Once your jars arrive, keep them in the fridge. Our guide on how to store TropiBlend after opening has tips to keep them tasting fresh.

Frequently asked questions

What is TropiBlend's contact number?

Call or text 0956 447 9961. You can also message TropiBlendph on Facebook or email elsie@tropiblend.com.

Can I order TropiBlend online?

Yes. Order through Facebook Messenger, by email, or with the order form on tropiblend.com. We confirm every order personally.

How do I pay for my TropiBlend order?

We'll share payment options when we confirm your order and price.
