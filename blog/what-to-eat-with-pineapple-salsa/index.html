What to Eat With Pineapple Salsa: Fish, Tacos, Pork and More

September 29, 2026 · 3 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

Pineapple salsa goes best with grilled fish, shrimp tacos, grilled and fried pork like liempo or lechon kawali, and as a dip with tortilla chips or crackers. Its sweet-and-tangy pineapple flavor balances salty, smoky and fatty food, making it a natural partner for Filipino grilled dishes.

Pineapple and pork are an old friendship in Filipino cooking. Think of pineapple in hamonado, or pineapple juice in BBQ marinade. TropiBlend Pineapple Salsa brings that same sweet-tangy lift, fresh and ready to spoon. Here's what to serve it with.

Our three favorite ways

These are the three uses on our Pineapple Salsa guide:

  1. Top it on grilled fish. It adds a sweet and tangy kick to grilled tilapia, bangus, tuna or any white fish.
  2. Perfect with tacos. Grilled shrimp tacos with pineapple salsa bring fresh, fruity flavor.
  3. Enjoy as a dip. It's great with chips, crackers or your favorite snacks.

More pairings to try

Lechon kawali and crispy pata. The acidity cuts through rich, crackly pork, a lighter alternative to the usual liver sauce.

Grilled liempo and pork BBQ. Sweet pineapple against smoky charred pork is a classic combination. Serve it with pickled onions for a complete inihaw condiment spread.

Grilled chicken inasal-style. Spoon salsa over the chicken, or eat a little with each bite of rice.

Burgers and hotdogs. Swap the relish for pineapple salsa for a tropical twist.

Ham and cheese sandwiches. Pineapple and ham are a tried-and-true combination.

Quesadillas. Serve a bowl of salsa on the side for dipping.

Serving tips

  • Spoon it on at the end. Add salsa after cooking, not during, so the pineapple stays fresh and bright.
  • Drain for tacos and sandwiches. Tilt the spoon against the jar to leave excess juice behind.
  • Use the juice. The leftover liquid makes a quick marinade base for chicken or pork.

Frequently asked questions

What meat goes with pineapple salsa?

Pork is the classic match: grilled liempo, BBQ, lechon kawali and ham. It's also excellent with grilled fish, shrimp and chicken.

Can pineapple salsa be used as a dip?

Yes. TropiBlend Pineapple Salsa is ready to serve as a dip with tortilla chips, crackers or other snacks.
