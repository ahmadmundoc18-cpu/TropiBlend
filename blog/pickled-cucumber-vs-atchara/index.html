Pickled Cucumber vs Atchara: What's the Difference?

September 29, 2026 · 2 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

Atchara is the classic Filipino pickle made mainly from grated green papaya and is usually sweet. TropiBlend Pickled Cucumber is made from crunchy Japanese cucumber with dill and lime, so it's crisper, fresher and more herby. Both do the same job on the plate, cutting through rich, grilled and fried food, so you can use pickled cucumber anywhere you'd serve atchara.

Every Filipino table knows atchara (also spelled atsara), the side dish that shows up next to lechon, fried fish and BBQ. So where does a jar of pickled cucumber fit?

What is atchara?

Atchara is a traditional Filipino pickle made mainly from grated unripe (green) papaya, often with carrots, bell pepper, ginger, garlic and raisins, in a sweet vinegar brine. It's sweet, tangy and a little crunchy.

What is TropiBlend Pickled Cucumber?

TropiBlend Pickled Cucumber is made with Japanese cucumber, dill and lime, plus cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices. It comes in a 500 ml jar.

  • Crunchier: cucumber keeps a satisfying snap
  • Fresher and herby: dill and lime give it a clean, cool taste
  • Bigger pieces: easy to layer on burgers and sandwiches

When to use each

Both play the same role: cutting through rich, salty, fried or grilled food.

  • Lechon, crispy pata, fried fish: both work well
  • Inihaw and lechon manok: pickled cucumber's cool crunch is a great match. See the best condiments for inihaw
  • Burgers and sandwiches: pickled cucumber works better here, since it lies flat and adds crunch
  • Rice meals and baon: either one

Can I use pickled cucumber instead of atchara?

Yes. Anywhere you'd put atchara on the plate, pickled cucumber does the same job with a fresher, crunchier taste. Many families keep both in the fridge, and add pickled onions for color and extra tang.

Frequently asked questions

Is atchara the same as pickles?

Atchara is a type of pickle, but it's specifically the Filipino style made from green papaya. "Pickles" in general can be made from cucumbers, onions and other vegetables.

What does TropiBlend Pickled Cucumber taste like?

Crisp, cool and tangy, with dill and lime.

What goes well with pickled cucumber?

Grilled chicken, lechon manok, pork BBQ, fried fish, burgers, sandwiches and rice meals.
