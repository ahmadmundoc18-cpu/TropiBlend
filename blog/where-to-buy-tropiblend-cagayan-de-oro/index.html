TropiBlend is a small, local brand, so you won't find us on every supermarket shelf yet. Getting a jar is still easy. Here are all the ways to buy TropiBlend in Cagayan de Oro.

1. Order directly from us (the easiest way)

Most of our customers order directly. Send us the products and quantities you'd like through any of these:

  • Facebook Messenger: m.me/TropiBlendph
  • Call or text: 0956 447 9961
  • Email: elsie@tropiblend.com
  • Order form: fill in the form on our website, which opens your email app with your order ready to send.

We'll reply to confirm availability, price and pickup or delivery details within Cagayan de Oro. It helps to include your barangay or area (for example Carmen, Lapasan or Downtown) so we can sort out the details quickly.

2. Visit us at local fairs and pop-ups

We love meeting customers in person, and fairs are the best place to taste before you buy. In September 2026 we joined the MSME fair at SM CDO Downtown, starting September 9.

Fairs and bazaars come and go, so the best way to find our next booth is to follow TropiBlendph on Facebook. We post where we'll be and when.

If you're planning to stock up at a fair, message us ahead of time and we can set aside the flavors you want.

3. Ask about larger orders

Buying for a party, an office or gifts? Message us with what you need and your date, and we'll let you know what we can do. See ordering TropiBlend in bulk for events and gifts.
