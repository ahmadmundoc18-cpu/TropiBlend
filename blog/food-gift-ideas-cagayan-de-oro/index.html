Food Gift and Pasalubong Ideas From Cagayan de Oro

September 29, 2026 · 3 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

For a unique pasalubong or food gift from Cagayan de Oro, try locally made gourmet condiments like TropiBlend's Mango Salsa, Pineapple Salsa, Pickled Cucumber, Pickled Onion, Pesto and Mango Salad Dressing. They're made in CDO from fresh, natural ingredients, look good in their labeled jars, and are something the recipient will actually use. Order by Messenger or at 0956 447 9961.

Pasalubong is how Filipinos share a place with the people they love. If you want to bring home something from Cagayan de Oro that's a little different, locally made condiments are worth a look.

Why condiments make great gifts

  • They get used. Unlike decorative souvenirs, a jar of salsa or pesto ends up on the dinner table.
  • They're made locally. TropiBlend is crafted in CDO, so your gift supports a Kagay-anon small business.
  • They look good. Our labeled jars already look like a gift, especially in a set.
  • They suit almost everyone. There's something for the pasta lover, the inihaw fan and the salad eater.

Gift ideas by person

For the home cook: Pesto and Mango Salad Dressing. Tuck in our 12 ways to use pesto as a recipe card.

For the grill master: Pickled Onion, Pickled Cucumber and Pineapple Salsa, a full inihaw condiment set.

For the snacker: Mango Salsa and Pineapple Salsa with a bag of tortilla chips. See what to eat with mango salsa.

For the balikbayan or visitor: A mix of all six, so they can try the full range.

Gifting tips

  1. Order ahead. Message us with your date and quantities, especially for multiple gifts. See bulk orders for events and gifts.
  2. Keep it cool. TropiBlend is best kept refrigerated because we don't use artificial preservatives. For travel, pack jars in a cooler bag and refrigerate on arrival.
  3. Add a serving note. Tuck in a card with a pairing idea, for example "Mango Salsa: try it on grilled fish!"

Traveling by plane? Check your airline's rules for liquids and jars in hand-carry. Checked baggage is usually the safer option for jars.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good pasalubong from Cagayan de Oro?

Locally made food products are a thoughtful choice. TropiBlend makes gourmet salsas, pickles, pesto and mango dressing in CDO from fresh, natural ingredients.

Can I bring TropiBlend on a trip?

Yes, but keep the jars cool. Pack them in a cooler bag and refrigerate as soon as you can, and check airline rules for liquids.

Can I order several jars as gifts?

Yes. Message us with the products, quantities and the date you need them.
