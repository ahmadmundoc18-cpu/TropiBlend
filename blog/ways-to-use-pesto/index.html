How to Use Pesto: 12 Ideas Beyond Pasta

September 29, 2026 · 3 min read · By TropiBlend

Quick answer

Besides pasta, use pesto as a spread on toast, pandesal and sandwiches, a marinade or topping for grilled chicken, pork and fish, a pizza sauce, a stir-in for scrambled eggs and soups, a dip mixed with mayo or cream cheese, and a dressing for potato salad or roasted vegetables. TropiBlend Pesto is made with basil, parmesan, extra virgin olive oil and cashew nuts.

Pesto pasta is delicious, but if that's the only way you use your jar, you're missing out. TropiBlend Pesto (basil, parmesan, extra virgin olive oil and cashew nuts) can upgrade breakfast, lunch, merienda and dinner.

Breakfast and merienda

  1. Pesto pandesal. Split warm pandesal and spread with pesto. Add cheese if you like. See our pesto pandesal ideas.
  2. Pesto toast. Toast bread, spread pesto generously, top with tomato and cheese. This is our Pesto made simple guide.
  3. Pesto eggs. Swirl a spoonful into scrambled eggs just before they set, or drizzle over a fried egg.
  4. Pesto grilled cheese. Spread thinly inside a cheese sandwich before toasting.

Lunch and dinner

  1. Quick pasta. Toss hot pasta with pesto and a splash of pasta water. Here's our 15-minute pesto pasta.
  2. Grilled chicken. Brush pesto over chicken during the last minutes of grilling, or spoon it on after.
  3. Pork chops and fish. Spoon a little over grilled pork chops or pan-fried fish fillets.
  4. Homemade pizza. Use pesto instead of tomato sauce, then add cheese and toppings.
  5. Soup booster. Stir a spoonful into vegetable, bean or tomato soup right before serving.

Dips and sides

  1. Pesto mayo. Mix equal parts pesto and mayonnaise for a sandwich spread or fries dip.
  2. Pesto cream cheese dip. Blend pesto with cream cheese and serve with crackers.
  3. Potato salad or roasted vegetables. Toss boiled potatoes or roasted vegetables with pesto while warm.

A little goes a long way. Pesto is rich and full of flavor, so start with a spoonful and add more to taste.

Allergy note: TropiBlend Pesto contains cashew nuts and dairy (parmesan). See our allergen guide.

Frequently asked questions

What can I eat with pesto besides pasta?

Toast, pandesal, sandwiches, grilled chicken, fish, pork, pizza, eggs, soups, potato salad and roasted vegetables.

Can you cook with pesto?

Yes, but add it at the end. High heat can make basil taste bitter and dull its color, so stir pesto in after cooking or brush it on in the last minutes.

Does TropiBlend Pesto contain nuts?

Yes. It contains cashew nuts as well as parmesan cheese.
