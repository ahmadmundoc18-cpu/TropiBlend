Our gourmet condiments

Six small-batch favorites, each made from fresh, natural ingredients. Every jar lists its full ingredients on the label, and there's nothing artificial in any of them.

  • Salsa

    Pineapple Salsa

    500 ml jar

    Sweet pineapple with crisp bell pepper and onion, brightened with lime. Scoop it up with chips or spoon it over grilled pork and fish.

    Best with: chips, grilled pork, fish, tacos

    Ingredients

    Pineapple, bell pepper, onion, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

  • Salsa

    Mango Salsa

    500 ml jar

    Ripe mango with bell pepper, onion and a squeeze of lime. It's a sweet and tangy topping for grilled salmon, chicken and seafood.

    Best with: grilled fish, chicken, seafood, chips

    Ingredients

    Mango, bell pepper, onion, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

  • Pickles

    Pickled Cucumber

    500 ml jar

    Crunchy Japanese cucumber pickled with dill and lime. It's a cool, bright side for inihaw, lechon manok and rice meals.

    Best with: grilled chicken, burgers, rice meals

    Ingredients

    Japanese cucumber, dill, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

  • Pickles

    Pickled Onion

    350 ml jar

    Bright, tangy pink onion rings with a zesty kick. They're the perfect condiment for every bite.

    Best with: grilled meats, fried chicken, burgers, sandwiches, tacos

    Ingredients

    Onion, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

  • Pesto

    Pesto

    300 ml jar

    Fragrant basil blended with parmesan, extra virgin olive oil and cashew nuts. Toss it with pasta, spread it on toast or spoon it over grilled meats.

    Best with: pasta, bread, grilled meats

    Ingredients

    Basil, parmesan cheese, extra virgin olive oil, cashew nuts, herbs and spices.

    Contains: tree nuts (cashew) and dairy.

  • Dressing

    Mango Salad Dressing

    350 ml bottle

    Silky mango and lime with a gentle pepper finish. It brings sunshine to green salads, avocado and grilled chicken bowls.

    Best with: green salads, avocado, chicken bowls

    Ingredients

    Mango, lime, cane sugar, salt, pepper, herbs and spices.

Our story

A taste of the tropics, made in Cagayan de Oro

TropiBlend is a Filipino food brand from Cagayan de Oro City, the City of Golden Friendship. We make gourmet salsas, pickles, pesto and salad dressing that bring bold, bright flavor to the dishes Filipinos already love, from inihaw and fried chicken to pasta and fresh salads.

Every jar starts with fresh fruit, vegetables and herbs, including sweet mangoes, ripe pineapples, crisp cucumbers and fragrant basil. We blend them in small batches with simple pantry ingredients like lime, cane sugar, salt and spices. There are no artificial preservatives, just real flavor you can taste.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I buy TropiBlend in Cagayan de Oro?

Visit our booth at the MSME fair at SM CDO Downtown (starting September 9, 2026). You can also order directly by messaging TropiBlendph on Facebook, calling or texting 0956 447 9961, or emailing elsie@tropiblend.com.

How do I order?

Send us the products and quantities you'd like on Facebook Messenger, by phone or by email. You can also use the order form below. We'll confirm availability, price and pickup or delivery details within Cagayan de Oro.

Are TropiBlend products all natural?

Yes. Every TropiBlend product is made from fresh and natural ingredients, with no artificial preservatives. The full ingredient list is printed on each label and shown on each product above.

Do any products contain allergens?

Our Pesto contains cashew nuts and parmesan cheese (dairy). If you have food allergies, please check each product's ingredient list. You're always welcome to message us with questions.

What food goes well with TropiBlend?

Try the salsas with chips, grilled fish and chicken; the pickles with grilled meats, fried chicken, burgers and rice meals; the pesto with pasta and toast; and the mango dressing with fresh green salads. For more ideas, see our guides to mango salsa, pineapple salsa, pickled onions and pesto.
