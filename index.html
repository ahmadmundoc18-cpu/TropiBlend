A taste of the tropics, made in Cagayan de Oro.

TropiBlend is a Filipino food brand from Cagayan de Oro City, the City of Golden Friendship. We make gourmet salsas, pickles, pesto and salad dressing that bring bold, bright flavor to the dishes Filipinos already love, from inihaw and fried chicken to pasta and fresh salads.

Every jar starts with fresh fruit, vegetables and herbs: sweet mangoes, ripe pineapples, crisp cucumbers and fragrant basil. We blend them in small batches with simple pantry ingredients like vinegar, citrus, cane sugar, salt and spices. There are no artificial preservatives, just real flavor you can taste.

Where can I buy TropiBlend in Cagayan de Oro?

Visit our booth at the MSME fair at SM CDO Downtown (starting September 9, 2026). You can also order online, message TropiBlendph on Facebook, call or text 0956 447 9961, or email elsie@tropiblend.com.

How do I order?

Add jars to your cart in the shop, or send us the products, sizes and quantities you'd like on Facebook Messenger, by phone or by email. You can also use the order form below. We'll confirm availability and pickup or delivery details within Cagayan de Oro.

What sizes and prices are there?

Every product comes in two sizes. Pineapple Salsa (₱385 / ₱295), Mango Salsa (₱485 / ₱385) and Pickled Cucumber (₱355 / ₱255) come in 500 ml and 350 ml jars. Pickled Onion (₱295 / ₱225) and Pesto (₱605 / ₱375) come in 350 ml and 200 ml jars. Mango Salad Dressing (₱495 / ₱325) comes in 350 ml and 150 ml bottles.

Are TropiBlend products all natural?

Yes. Every TropiBlend product is made from fresh and natural ingredients, with no artificial preservatives. The full ingredient list is printed on each label and shown under each product above.

Do any products contain allergens?

Our Pesto contains cashew nuts and parmesan cheese (dairy). If you have food allergies, please check each product's ingredient list. You're always welcome to message us with questions.

What food goes well with TropiBlend?

Try the salsas with chips, grilled fish and chicken; the pickles with grilled meats, fried chicken, burgers and rice meals; the pesto with pasta and toast; and the mango dressing with fresh green salads. For more ideas, see our guides to mango salsa, pineapple salsa, pickled onions and pesto.
